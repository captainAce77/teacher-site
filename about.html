Background

My name is Yusuf Ibrahim, also known as Captain Ace. I am a Business Education graduate with a specialization in Accounting and a passionate educator dedicated to helping students succeed in Financial Accounting and Commerce.

Teaching Experience

I currently teach at Gigatt Private Schools, where I prepare students for academic excellence and external examinations. Before joining Gigatt Private Schools, I taught at Sydney International School, gaining valuable classroom experience and developing effective teaching strategies.

Professional Development

I am a Student Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) and currently progressing through the Foundation Level. I am committed to continuous learning and professional growth in accounting and finance.

Financial Advisory Experience

In addition to teaching, I have worked as a Financial Advisor with Heirs Life Assurance, helping clients understand financial planning, insurance, and wealth protection. This experience allows me to connect classroom learning with real-world financial practices.

My Mission

My goal is to make accounting and commerce easy to understand, practical, and enjoyable for students. Through this website, I share lesson notes and learning resources to support students, teachers, and parents.
